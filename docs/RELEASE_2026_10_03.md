# Certified metric scopes — 3 October 2026

The keyless app can answer denial and net collection rate questions with one
payer, payer-type or provider-specialty breakdown and one exact catalog-value
filter. The underlying policy-owned SQL formula is retained. Denial rate uses
adjudicated Paid/Denied claims; collection rate uses paid claims only.

Examples:

- What is the claim denial rate by payer type?
- What is the net collection rate for Medicare?
- What is the claim denial rate by payer type for Cardiology?
- What is the net collection rate for payer Blue Cross Blue Shield by specialty?

## Boundaries

Every requested qualifier must be supported. Multiple metrics, additional
filters, rankings, date windows and unknown catalog values are refused. Matching
is exact after case and punctuation normalization; no model or fuzzy matching
selects a definition or value. Dimension keys must be unique before a join can
compose a rate. Left joins retain unmatched facts in unfiltered breakdowns.

Catalog reads, join-integrity checks and answer queries use the caller's access
scope and request deadline. The query projects only the formula's required
columns. Both SQL and result verification now gate the certified app path:
blocking findings prevent prose, speech and result-table publication.

A scoped result keeps the metric owner and definition, and states its filter
and breakdown. It does not claim to match the committed overall benchmark.
